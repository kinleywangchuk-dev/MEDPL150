Reflection

I chose this photo because it captures a powerful contrast between nature and urban life. The city lights create a sense of energy and human activity, while the mountain in the background feels calm, massive, and timeless. My composition idea focused on balance and depth the bright, detailed foreground leads the viewer’s eye upward toward the mountain, creating a layered effect.I also liked how the colors transition from cool city tones to the warmer color on the mountain, giving the image a natural visual flow and a sense of scale.
