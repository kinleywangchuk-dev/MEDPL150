"Burning Home"

They did not start the fire yet they are consumed by it. The polar bear stands at the center of a flame, not as a symbol of strength, but as a warning. The Arctic, once a kingdom of endless ice, is now the fastest-warming region on Earth heating four times more rapidly than the global average. For polar bears, this isn't a distant forecast. It's the present. As sea ice vanishes, so does their hunting ground. Without ice, there are no seals. Without seals, there are no bears. The cub beside her mother doesn't know that the world being handed to her is smaller, warmer, and more fragile than the one before. We built the fire. We can choose to put it out.
